# Pipsey brand and selected mascot

## Personality
Pipsey is a little curious robot that peeks over the edge of your screen, with glowing cyan mint eyes and an integrated glowing camera aperture shutter on its forehead. Warm, playful, observant, and technically competent; not a generic corporate AI bot. It should make “Hey Pipsey, can you explain this?” feel natural.

## Owner-selected logo & Visual Identity
The visual identity features the owner-selected robot mascot:
- Pearlescent white helmet dome with side headphones and twin antenna leaves.
- Glowing cyan/emerald camera aperture shutter lens on the forehead representing visual perception.
- Large glowing cyan oval eyes and white paws resting over the bottom shelf.
- Native macOS application icon packaged in an Apple-standard squircle badge with transparent outer vertices (`alpha = 0`).

## Motion rules
- **Idle Peeking Cadence:** 8 seconds of active curiosity animation (looking left, right, and upwards) followed by a 30-second resting phase.
- **Ambient Cyan Breathing Aura:** A continuous, gentle 3.2-second sinusoidal glow around the mascot in floating pebble mode.
- **Listening & Capturing:** Pulsing ruby recording jewel with radial glow bloom and live 5-bar reactive cyan equalizer.
- **Telescope Mode:** Rotating optical sweep radar ring around the mascot with cyan viewfinder crosshair indicator.
- **Processing:** Truthful feedback driven by actual inference state (`HostedVLMClient`), never simulated completion.
- **Speaking:** Native TTS playback with synced audio equalizer activity. Reduced-motion mode supported.
