# Pipsey brand and selected mascot

## Personality
Pipsey is a little curious robot that peeks over the edge of your screen, with bright mint eyes. Warm, playful, observant, and technically competent; not a generic corporate AI bot. It should make “Hey Pipsey, can you explain this?” feel natural.

## Motion rules
Idle: calm/eyes only. Listening: subtle eye reaction plus real mic indicator. Capturing: unmistakable, accessible recording status with elapsed timer. Processing: truthful feedback driven by actual inference state, never simulated completion. Speaking: minimal synced animation if audio actually plays. Reduced-motion mode supported.

## Usage
README: centered peeking robot above “Pipsey” and tagline. Inside the Mac island: prefer mascot eye/visor silhouette to the whole wordmark. App launcher icon: remove in-icon wordmark for legibility at small sizes and export multiple scale variants. Keep natural clear space. Don't use official HeyClicky marks or imply a partnership.
